The honest bit

Motion prompts

The prompts behind the motion.

Nothing hidden. These are the motion prompts I use to bring real food photos to life: one gentle move per shot, slow and natural.

Every prompt ends with the same rule: keep the dish exactly as it is. Same food, same portion, same plate. Nothing added, nothing removed.

  • Banh mi · Footscray

    Motion prompt

    Subtle handheld feel, gentle sway. The train moves slowly across the bridge in the soft-focus background while cars pass on the road below. Herbs in the roll flutter very slightly and warm sunset light flickers across the bread. The banh mi stays sharp and centred.

  • Ethiopian feast · Footscray

    Motion prompt

    Slow drift from overhead to a low angle across the platter. Hands reach in naturally and tear injera while friends laugh and move gently. A tram glides past in the background and fairy lights twinkle. The platter stays exactly as it is.

  • Filipino bakery · Sunshine

    Motion prompt

    Slow dolly push along the glass counter toward the pandesal bags. A warm heat haze rises from the fresh bread rolls. Staff move naturally and softly out of focus behind the counter.

  • Chef's plate · Maidstone

    Motion prompt

    Locked-off camera. Q slowly places one microgreen with tweezers onto the salmon, then lifts his hand away. Faint steam rises from the fish. The kitchen stays softly out of focus and the plate stays unchanged.

  • Pho · Footscray

    Motion prompt

    Slow push-in toward the bowl. Thin wisps of steam rise and curl. Chopsticks slowly lift a few noodles from the broth, which ripples gently. The herbs stay fresh and still.

  • Dumplings · Braybrook

    Motion prompt

    Locked-off camera. Steam rises from the open bamboo steamer. Chopsticks slowly lift one dumpling, which wobbles gently. The other dumplings stay perfectly still.

  • Butter chicken · Sunshine

    Motion prompt

    Slow push-in. Steam rises from the curry and the sauce glistens, moving very slightly. A hand tears the naan softly in the background.

  • Pide · Maidstone

    Motion prompt

    Slow 20-degree orbit at table height. The cheese bubbles gently, then a slice is lifted away with a slow, stretchy cheese pull.

  • Cafe brunch · Maidstone

    Motion prompt

    Slow push-in to a close-up. A knife slices slowly into the poached egg and the yolk runs gently across the avocado.
