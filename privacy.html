Who I am

Plated Media Co (ABN 44 530 379 582) is run by me, Q, from Maidstone, Victoria. When this page says "I" or "me", that's who it means.

What I collect

When you ask for a free poster, I collect:

  • your name and your business name
  • your suburb
  • your email address or phone number
  • the photos of your food you choose to send

Why I collect it

Only to make your free poster and to get in touch with you about it. If you go on to order a service, I'll use the same details to make and deliver your ads.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell your details. Ever.
  • I don't add you to mailing lists without asking.
  • I don't use your photos or your ad in my own portfolio or social posts unless you say that's OK.

Where it's stored

The form on this site is handled by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which emails your message and photos straight to my inbox. FormSubmit doesn't keep a copy of your photos. I keep your details only as long as I need them to make your ad and look after you as a customer.

Your choices

You can ask to see the details I hold about you, ask me to fix them, or ask me to delete them at any time. Just email platedmediaco@gmail.com and I'll sort it.

Questions
